# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ClipRelay is for an individual creator or channel operator who keeps a large local video archive on macOS or Windows. They need to browse or randomly select a clip, make a quick trim, fit it to delivery limits, and publish it without juggling a general-purpose editor, Finder or File Explorer, Telegram, and multiple caption drafts.

## Product Purpose

ClipRelay turns a nested local video archive into a reliable posting workflow. It can choose a random clip immediately without waiting for a full visual index, validates media before preparation, supports deliberate browsing, creates safe derivative files, sends through a Telegram bot or personal account, prepares a near-frictionless manual X post, and records what happened. Success means the source archive is never modified, optional background analysis never blocks Random, every delivery has a clear state, and a routine post can be prepared in a few focused actions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and tactile. The voice is concise and specific, with the confidence of a professional media tool and the friendliness of a personal utility. Progress and errors should feel transparent rather than technical or alarming.

## Anti-references

ClipRelay must not feel like a generic corporate dashboard, a neon gaming interface, an overdecorated AI SaaS product, a miniature nonlinear video editor, or a disguised system file picker. It must avoid modal-heavy workflows, cropped media thumbnails, vague status labels, decorative motion, and destructive cleanup that could ever touch source videos.

## Design Principles

1. **The video leads.** Media, timing, and delivery state receive the strongest hierarchy; interface chrome stays quiet.
2. **One continuous workflow.** Random selection and library browsing converge on the same prepare, publish, and history path.
3. **Make irreversible actions unnecessary.** Originals are read-only, generated files move to Trash, and partial delivery states remain recoverable.
4. **Show the real state.** Scanning, conversion, Telegram delivery, and manual X completion each expose precise progress and actionable failures.
5. **Power without editor sprawl.** Trimming and compression are direct, destination-aware tools rather than a general-purpose editing suite.
6. **Large libraries stay interactive.** Startup and browsing use paged database reads with virtualized tiles; filename discovery, metadata verification, thumbnails, and hover previews remain separate background stages. Only the chosen clip must be validated before preparation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA-equivalent behavior for the desktop interface. Every hover action has a keyboard or click alternative; focus is always visible; controls provide at least 44 by 44 logical-pixel targets; status is never communicated by color alone; motion is brief and nonessential; text and controls remain usable at 200% scaling; and hover previews are muted.
